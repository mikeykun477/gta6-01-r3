VELORIA

STREETS OF THE NEON TIDE

Ten years ago Jax Corvo drove the getaway car for the biggest score Veloria ever saw — then vanished. Now he is back, broke, and Mama Odette owns his debt. One city. Six jobs. A syndicate that does not forgive. Steal cars, outrun the Veloria PD, and take the streets back, one mission at a time.

An open-world crime game: explore Veloria on foot and by car, steal anything with wheels, lose the law at 5 wanted stars, and run six story missions — plus courier side-jobs for cash.
